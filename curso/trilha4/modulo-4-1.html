MÓDULO 4.1

🛍️ Propaganda e produto

Seis receitas de still publicitário. Cada uma com prompt EN pronto + ferramenta recomendada + variações.

1🍔 Anúncio de fast food

MJ v7 ou Flux

Hero do hambúrguer dominando o quadro, gradiente escuro top/bottom pra texto, headline pronta pra entrar.

hero food photography of a juicy double cheeseburger with melted cheddar dripping, sesame brioche bun freshly baked, lettuce and tomato peeking, dramatic studio lighting from above, deep brown gradient background fading to black at top and bottom, hyper detailed texture, shallow depth of field 85mm macro, vibrant amber and crimson palette, hunger-inducing appetizing mood --ar 9:16 --no text, watermark, blurry

Variações: trocar burger por pizza, sushi, sorvete. Trocar ângulo (top-down para layout flat).

2👜 Produto premium em fundo escuro

Flux 1.1 Pro

Estética minimalista de luxo. Sujeito centralizado, fundo preto profundo, rim light de cor única.

minimalist luxury product photography of [PRODUCT, e.g. leather handbag in deep oxblood], floating against pure black void background, single soft rim light from upper-left creating subtle highlights, shot on Phase One 100mm macro, ultra-sharp detail, dramatic chiaroscuro, refined sophisticated mood, magazine quality --ar 4:5 --no clutter, no shadow on background, no logo, no text

Variações: relógio, perfume, joia, garrafa. Trocar cor do rim light (azul cyan, dourado, magenta).

3🏃 Lifestyle ad outdoor

Runway Gen-4 ou MJ v7

Pessoa real usando produto em cenário aspiracional. Foco no sentimento, não no objeto.

aspirational lifestyle photograph of a 30-year-old athletic woman running across a coastal trail at golden hour, wearing premium sportswear [BRAND], sweat glistening on her skin, expression of focused joy, ocean and cliffs in soft background bokeh, shot on 85mm f/1.8, warm cinematic color grade, Nike-style commercial aesthetic, motion blur subtle on legs --ar 4:5 --no stiff pose, no studio look, no fake smile

Variações: yoga matinal, escalada, ciclismo urbano, surfe. Tonificar para mood do produto.

4💄 Beauty close-up

MJ v7 ou Flux

Pele perfeita, foco em detalhe de maquiagem ou produto. Iluminação de catálogo Vogue.

editorial beauty close-up of a woman's face, focus on her glossy ruby lips and subtle gold eye makeup, soft butterfly lighting from front with gentle fill, flawless dewy skin, freckles visible, ethereal mood, shot on Hasselblad with 120mm macro, Vogue editorial style, fashion photography 2026, pastel rose background gradient --ar 4:5 --no oversmoothing, no plastic skin, no airbrushed look

Variações: foco em cabelo, perfume sendo aplicado, base, fragrance. Mudar mood (etéreo / editorial / urban).

5📱 Tech gadget em mesa minimalista

Flux 1.1 Pro

Estética Apple/Nothing/Teenage Engineering. Top-down ou 3/4, fundo limpo, paleta minimalista.

premium tech product photography of [DEVICE, e.g. minimalist wireless earbuds in matte white], floating slightly above a clean concrete textured surface, top-down 3/4 angle, soft diffused studio light with no harsh shadows, monochromatic palette with single accent color reflection, Apple keynote aesthetic, ultra-sharp detail on textures, 50mm macro --ar 4:5 --no clutter, no other objects, no reflection of room

Variações: fone, smartwatch, smartphone, smart speaker. Adicionar 1 acessório (cabo, case) para storytelling.

6🥤 Bebida com splash e gelo

MJ v7 ou Flux

High-speed photography. Líquido em movimento, gotas suspensas, gelo cristalino. Conta a "sensação".

high-speed commercial photography of an icy [DRINK, e.g. citrus iced tea] being poured into a tall glass, vibrant amber liquid splashing dynamically, large ice cubes suspended mid-fall, fresh lime slices and mint leaves frozen in air, condensation droplets on glass, white seamless studio background, hard rim light from behind catching the splashes, Pepsi-style commercial aesthetic, 100mm macro frozen at 1/8000s --ar 4:5 --no spilling on table, no blur

Variações: refrigerante, suco, cerveja com espuma, café cold brew. Mudar acessórios (fruta, especiaria, gelo).
