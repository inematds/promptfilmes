🎬 Cinema e curta

Seis estéticas cinematográficas prontas. Cola, troca o sujeito, gera. Cada uma com referência de filme/diretor que ativa o look.

1🕵️ Noir chuvoso

MJ v7 + Sora 2

Detetive solitário em rua molhada, neon refletindo no asfalto. Sin City + Blade Runner.

cinematic film noir scene of a solitary detective in a long trench coat walking down a rain-soaked alley at night, neon signs reflecting in puddles, smoke from a manhole, single bare bulb above a doorway, high contrast chiaroscuro, deep blacks and stark whites, Cinestill 800T film stock with characteristic orange halation around lights, shot on anamorphic 35mm 2.39:1, in the style of Sin City meets Blade Runner, melancholic mood --ar 21:9 --no text, watermark, modern cars

Variações: trocar detetive por femme fatale, jornalista, fugitivo. Mudar cor dominante do neon.

2🤖 Sci-fi cyberpunk Blade Runner

Sora 2 ou Veo 3

Cidade vertical com chuva ácida, holograms gigantes, paleta neon roxo/cyan.

cyberpunk cityscape at night, towering brutalist skyscrapers with giant holographic advertisements in Japanese kanji, acid rain falling, neon magenta and cyan lighting dominant, dense fog at street level, a lone figure in a long coat walking through steam vents, flying vehicles in distant background, in the style of Blade Runner 2049 cinematography by Roger Deakins, anamorphic lens flares, shot on Arri Alexa, 2.39:1 ratio --ar 21:9 --no people in foreground, no daylight

Variações: interior do bar do filme, mercado de rua noturno, vista do alto. Trocar paleta dominante.

3🎞️ Drama íntimo Kodak Portra

MJ v7 ou Flux

Cena cotidiana com profundidade emocional. Luz natural, tons quentes desaturados, "vibe de filme A24".

intimate cinematic drama still of a mother and teenage daughter sitting silently at a kitchen table in late afternoon, soft warm window light streaming sideways, unresolved tension in their expressions, shot on Kodak Portra 400 film, 50mm shallow depth of field, muted earth tone palette, in the style of A24 cinematography, Greta Gerwig meets Sean Baker mood, documentary intimacy --ar 2:3 --no theatrical pose, no fake emotion

Variações: casal silencioso na cama, pai e filho no carro, idosos no parque. Manter intimismo.

4😈 Thriller psicológico Fincher

Flux 1.1 Pro

Verde-amarelo doente, simetria gélida, alto contraste. Seven + Zodiac.

psychological thriller scene of a detective examining evidence in a dimly lit basement office, single overhead fluorescent light, sickly green-yellow color grade, deep shadows, methodical symmetric composition, papers and photographs spread on a metal desk, high contrast dark cinematography, in the style of David Fincher (Seven, Zodiac), shot on Red camera digital aesthetic, 2.39:1, cold grim atmosphere --ar 21:9 --no warm tones, no smile, no clutter

Variações: cena de investigação em casa abandonada, sala de interrogatório, biblioteca à noite.

5🤠 Faroeste Deakins golden hour

Veo 3 ou Sora 2

Silhueta contra sol baixo, paisagem desértica vasta. Roger Deakins iluminando o vazio.

epic western landscape at golden hour, a lone cowboy on horseback silhouetted against an enormous orange sun setting behind distant red rock mesas, dust kicked up by hooves backlit, sparse Joshua trees, anamorphic 2.39:1 widescreen, in the style of Roger Deakins cinematography (Sicario, No Country for Old Men), shot on Arri Alexa with vintage anamorphic lenses, restricted earth-tone palette, mythic atmosphere --ar 21:9 --no modern elements, no other people

Variações: showdown na rua principal de cidade fantasma, traveling em carruagem, acampamento à fogueira.

6📼 Found footage VHS anos 80

MJ v7

Estética de gravação caseira analógica. Saturação esquisita, scan lines, timestamp queimado.

found footage VHS home video from 1986, suburban backyard birthday party at dusk, kids in awkward poses around a folding table with cake, slightly washed out colors with greenish tint, visible horizontal scan lines and chromatic aberration, timestamp burned in bottom-right "07-22-1986 18:43", harsh on-camera flash with red-eye, 4:3 aspect ratio cropped from original 16mm-look, Stranger Things season 1 era aesthetic --ar 4:3 --no modern items, no smartphones

Variações: gravação de casamento, festa de Halloween, viagem familiar de Kombi, festival de música.
